Privacy policy

rclone-drive-sync is used solely by its owner for personal Google Drive file management and synchronization.

Google Drive file contents and metadata are accessed only to perform operations requested by the owner. Downloaded files and authorization tokens are stored on the owner's devices. This website does not receive Drive data or tokens.

The owner does not sell Google user data or share it for advertising. Transfers occur between Google Drive and destinations configured by the owner.

Locally stored files remain until the owner deletes them. Access can be revoked through the owner's Google Account third-party connections settings, and locally stored rclone credentials can be removed from the owner's devices.

Contact: through the project's GitHub issues. Do not include private data.
